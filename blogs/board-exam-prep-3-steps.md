Most students open the textbook on page one and start reading. Three months later they are stuck in chapter four and panic begins. There is a better way. Here is the simple 3 step system we recommend for every Punjab board student, from Class 9 to 2nd year.

## Step 1: Fix your syllabus list first

Before studying anything, know exactly what is inside your paper. Boards change the syllabus, and old advice from seniors may no longer match your books.

- Open the [smart syllabus](/smart-syllabus/) page for your class and note the chapter list
- In your notebook, mark every chapter as **Strong**, **Average** or **Weak**
- Be honest. This one list will decide your whole study plan

Your weak chapters get the most time. Strong chapters only need quick revision. This simple step alone puts you ahead of most students.

## Step 2: Learn chapter by chapter, in order

For every chapter, follow the same small loop:

1. Read the **chapter notes** once, slowly, with a pencil in hand
2. Solve the **exercise solutions** yourself before checking the answers
3. Finish with the **MCQ bank** for that chapter

You will find all three on your [class page](/classes/). When one chapter is done, update your list: Weak becomes Average, Average becomes Strong.

> **Teacher tip:** two focused hours beat six tired hours. Phone in another room, one chapter target, then a real break.

## Step 3: Past papers every week

This is the step toppers never skip. From the very first month, attempt one [past paper](/past-papers/) every week under exam timing. No books, no phone, three hours.

- Check your paper honestly and note every mistake type
- Compare your answers with the [pairing scheme](/pairing-scheme/) to see where marks come from
- Two months before boards, add [guess papers](/guess-papers/) for expected questions
- Use our [online tests](/tests/) for quick self checks between papers

## A simple weekly routine

1. Monday to Friday: two chapters from Step 2, one hour each
2. Saturday: one full past paper with a timer
3. Sunday: check the paper, update your chapter list, rest

Follow this for 12 weeks and your notebook will tell you exactly how ready you are. No guessing, no panic.

## Final words

Board exams reward students who practice, not students who read the most pages. Start with Step 1 today; it takes only twenty minutes. If you want a plan for a specific subject, email us at admin.studysapiens@gmail.com and we may write a full guide for it.

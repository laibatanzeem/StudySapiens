Notes, past papers and pairing schemes already live on the [class pages](/classes/). But students ask us bigger questions every week. How many hours should I study daily? Which chapters matter most for the paper? How do I revise smartly in the last month? A short update post cannot answer these properly. So this blog is our home for **full, honest guides**.

## What you will find here

- **Study plans** for Class 9, Class 10, 1st year and 2nd year
- **Syllabus explainers**: what changed in the new PCTB books and what it means for your paper
- **Exam strategy**: pairing scheme tricks, paper presentation, time management
- **Entry test basics** for MDCAT, ECAT and CSS beginners

Every guide is written in simple English, for real students, for the current session.

## Our three honesty rules

1. **Facts are checked twice.** Dates, formulas and board rules are verified against official notifications before anything goes live.
2. **No rumours as news.** If a date sheet or result date is only expected, we clearly label it "Expected" until the board confirms it.
3. **Simple English only.** If a Class 9 student cannot understand a sentence, we rewrite that sentence.

We will never post copied content from other websites. Freeilm and IlmKiDunya already exist: our job is to explain things better, not to repeat them.

## Tell us what to write next

This blog grows with your questions. If a topic confuses you, email us at admin@studysapiens.com and a teacher may turn it into a full guide.

While you wait, you can try an [online MCQ test](/tests/) or open your [class page](/classes/) to see the latest verified uploads.

Punjab Boards & PECTAA Assessment Guidelines (2026-27)

These pairing schemes outline chapter-wise weightage for Objective (MCQs) and Subjective (Short & Long Questions) sections across all 9 BISE boards of Punjab (Lahore, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, Multan, Bahawalpur, D.G. Khan, and Sahiwal).

Always cross-check official curriculum updates and ALP notifications published by PECTAA before final exam preparation.

PECTAA Grade 9 Notice 1238 PECTAA Grade 11 Notice 1239 PECTAA Curriculum & Compliance

Objective Part

MCQs are spread across every unit of the new book. Our scheme cards mark the exact unit count so you never over-study a unit.

Short Questions

Boards give extra choice (e.g. attempt 8 of 12). The scheme shows which units feed each block: prepare blocks, not luck.

Long Question Pairing

Every long question joins TWO units. Learn the pairs early and practise combined answers: that's how 90+ papers are written.
